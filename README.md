### 🚀 About Me

I'm an AI/ML professional based in Alappuzha, Kerala, India, holding an **MSc in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning** and a **BSc in Cyber Forensics** (MG University). I worked as an AI & Machine Learning Faculty at **Evolve Robotics LLP**, teaching Python, ML and computer vision, mentoring students on end-to-end AI projects, and building a portfolio of AI/ML projects under the **EVOLVE** brand.
